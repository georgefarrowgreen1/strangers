From Green Beans to Final Roast

Carefully Sourced and Roasted

At our roastery, we meticulously sample every coffee before purchasing the raw green beans. Once we discover a coffee we love, we profile it on our roaster to achieve the optimal flavours. We analyse every batch of roasted coffee, ensuring exceptional flavour quality before release. This rigorous process guarantees that we maintain complete control over every aspect of our roasting, delivering the finest coffee to our customers.

Strangers Coffee Company - Award-Winning Coffee in Norwich

Strangers Coffee Company is an independent coffee brand with three locations across Norwich: a café, a roastery, and a takeaway site. Established in 2009, Strangers is firmly rooted in the city’s coffee scene and is known for serving only speciality-grade coffee, roasted all on-site at their roastery.

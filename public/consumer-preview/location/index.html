Coffee in Norwich

Discover Strangers Coffee Company, one of Norwich’s leading independent speciality coffee roasters and cafés. We’re passionate about serving exceptional coffee, responsibly sourced beans and expertly crafted espresso or filter drinks in the heart of Norwich.

Visit any of our Norwich coffee shop locations to enjoy freshly brewed coffee prepared by our knowledgeable baristas, whether you prefer an espresso, flat white, filter coffee or your ‘usual’. Our opening hours are listed below, making it easy to plan your next coffee break, catch-up or takeaway coffee stop.

Love the coffee you drink in our cafés? Take the experience home with our freshly roasted whole coffee beans or ground coffee, ideal for espresso machines, cafetières, pour-over brewing and other home brewing methods.

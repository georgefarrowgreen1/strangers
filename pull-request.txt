Admins can now add coffees and edit their names, origin, process, tasting notes, categories and independent 250 g/500 g/1 kg prices in Admin → Wholesale → Coffee catalogue. Manage up to six JPEG/PNG/WebP photos per coffee, upload or remove images, and restore bundled originals. Both the native editor and customer gallery preserve complete image proportions. Added coffees appear in the ordering page without a code change.

Visibility and stock are separate controls. Out-of-stock coffees remain visible with ordering disabled; hidden coffees leave the list. Checkout refreshes availability and prices, and the server refuses unavailable products or stale quotes. Existing successful retries keep their original result. Staff preview orders no longer inflate preparation totals.

Admins set each wholesale customer's payment terms and instructions. Customers see their read-only terms in My account, checkout, receipt and history; admin order details show the same snapshot. Terms default to To be agreed, with before-dispatch, 7/14/30/60-day invoice periods and Custom available. Customer profile writes cannot alter terms, and changed terms or business details require a new checkout review. Existing orders preserve their original prices, delivery details and terms. Saving details before reviewing an account preserves edited terms during registration approval.

Uses the existing versioned D1 records and FILES KV binding. No schema migration, secret, DNS or service changes. Uploaded photo bytes are outside JSON/D1 backups; documentation explains retaining originals and reuploading after restore. Payments, invoices, email and fulfilment remain manual.

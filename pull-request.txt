## Staff wholesale Royal Mail labels

Employees with Wholesale orders access can request and download a Royal Mail label for a ready delivery from the desktop packing desk or phone order details. Staff review the saved destination, the service configured by Admin and the sealed parcel weight before confirming. Reprinting uses the existing shipment. The order stays ready until staff confirm handover.

The Worker checks payment eligibility before booking, downloading the label and confirming Royal Mail handover. An account hold or overdue balance blocks shipment even if the current order is paid. Standard credit terms allow shipment within terms. Before-dispatch orders require confirmed settlement covering that order. When that decision depends on a QuickBooks balance, the balance must have been checked within the past 24 hours. Staff see the reason for a hold and can recheck after Admin reviews it.

## Test coverage

The release includes Worker tests for payment terms, settlement evidence, account-wide holds, permissions and changes during booking. Browser tests exercise the staff flow on desktop and phone, including reprinting, recovering an uncertain request and handling a payment hold before handover.

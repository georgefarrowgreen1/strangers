Employees can now manage leave alongside their rota: individual days-based balances, reviewed annual/unpaid requests, half-day absence times, a shared calendar of approved annual leave and visible blackout dates. Admins manage individual leave years/allowances, exceptional carry-over, private sickness/unauthorised absence, approvals, company/site capacity and bank-holiday days in lieu.

Approved absences use the existing rota_time_off source and its conflict/open-shift checks. Existing assigned shifts remain visible for admin resolution. Integer charges, saved reviewed values, idempotent creates, individual versions and a transactional global revision prevent silent overwrites, duplicate requests, overdrawn budgets and competing approvals exceeding capacity.

The stated policy defaults to 1 April–31 March, editable to 6 April or another agreed date. Inclusive bank holidays are reserved within entitlement; admin-confirmed worked dates release a day in lieu once, without adding the entitlement twice. Credits have audited corrections. A fixed public GOV.UK lookup optionally helps enter England/Wales dates without sending employee data. Its tested manual-entry fallback preserves the form and keeps the rota online when the optional feed is unavailable; real feed reachability was unavailable in the local Worker.

Migration 24 is additive. Existing time off stays private and unclassified until explicitly reconciled; opening used balances can cover historical records without charging them twice. No employee entitlement, carry-over, full-time status or Who’s Off data is invented or imported automatically. Requests start disabled until an admin sets the policy and individual records. All new tables are backed up. Setup and migration guidance is in docs/leave-management.md.

The existing role-downgrade browser regression now synchronises with the expected early security navigation, allowing only the destroyed-old-realm error while retaining the required safe navigation and private-data assertion.

The only new colleague-data access is an explicit calendar allowlist of approved annual-leave names/dates. Balances, pending requests, reasons, sickness/other absence, hours and private records remain employee/admin-only; administration/events are admin-only. Existing origin, identity, no-store and shared-session safeguards cover the new routes, and changing account clears private dashboards/forms.

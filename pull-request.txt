## Wholesale invoicing workspace

- Add Today, Billing accounts, Invoices, Collections and Reconciliation to Admin → Wholesale → Invoicing.
- Preview customer statements with verified balances and download the reviewed PDF. Review billing exceptions and payment reminders against the current account and invoice.
- Review dispatch batches and group monthly accounts. Track issuance, QuickBooks posting and optional email delivery after approval.
- Save dated month-end checklists, figures and notes. Cover every linked order in the invoice index and retain billing records in backups.
- Use the configured invoice number series for individual and batch issuance. Approved batches wait when their QuickBooks company is unavailable.

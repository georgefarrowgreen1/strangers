## Wholesale and account recovery

- Resolve customer order messages against the business's current order address. Recheck order status and email settings on dispatch retries. An address change after an unconfirmed attempt requires delivery review.
- Send password resets to the saved order address. Revoke outstanding reset links and invitations when that address changes.

## Staff and Admin

- Use working hours and actual absence times to choose open-shift recipients.
- Queue rota publication emails, Monday summaries and holiday reminders in D1. The existing scheduled job retries temporary failures. Migration 37 creates the staff email outbox.
- Distinguish queued, provider-accepted and delivered messages. Correct the wholesale labels in Activity, show full order references in Email health and check external DMARC authorisation.

Verified with 464 API and unit tests, 53 browser tests and a Worker build dry run. The production DNS host for `strangerscoffee.com` needs to publish the external DMARC authorisation TXT record separately.

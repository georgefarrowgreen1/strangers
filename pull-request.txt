## Consumer preview

Ships the approved retail homepage and stock-aware finder, 18 shareable product and gift pages, standalone guest and account checkout, private order tracking and improved My account. Consumer (Preview) gains a paginated packing and dispatch queue and a support inbox.

Retail service version 15 is deployed with additive migrations, immutable checkout records, authenticated fulfilment and a separate contact inbox. Payments and dispatch remain in the preview test flow. Retail routes cannot reach wholesale or QuickBooks.

Verified desktop and mobile Chromium, checkout retry and isolation through both Workers and D1, Admin packing, tracking and support, catalogue consistency, and a Wrangler deployment dry run.

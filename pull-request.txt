Customer-specific coffees are currently mixed into the general admin catalogue. Add Admin → Wholesale → Custom coffees, reusing the existing customer allowlists, searchable customer choices and versioned coffee editor. General catalogue rows exclude custom coffees, and existing assignments appear in the new section automatically.

New custom coffees start hidden. A saved private marker keeps a custom coffee private with no selected customers, including when the final assignment is removed from a legacy record. Only an explicit admin switch to general visibility clears that restriction. Catalogue, new-order and uploaded-photo checks share the same allowlist; customer payloads omit visibility metadata, other customer IDs and recipes. Existing orders retain their snapshots. No real customer assignments are changed and no database migration is required.

Order and invoice history also gain a larger rounded refresh arrow inside a themed 44 px circular button, preserving labelled focus, busy rotation, duplicate-request guards, retries and reduced-motion behaviour.

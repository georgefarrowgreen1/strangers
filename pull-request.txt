## Staff and Admin passkeys

Staff, managers and admins can enrol from the account menu in Kitchen Diary, Rota or Admin using their current password. Staff Login offers passkey sign-in and returns the person to the workspace they opened with their existing permissions. Password sign-in and the existing quick PIN rules remain available.

WebAuthn registration and sign-in run on the canonical staff origin. Migration 53 adds the credential and single-use challenge tables. Disabling an account or resetting its password from Admin revokes its credentials. The consumer and trade logins keep their existing independent authentication flows.

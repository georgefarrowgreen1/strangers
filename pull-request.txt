Forecasts now compare six small forecasting methods against earlier completed weeks instead of applying the same recent-sales model to every coffee. This supports repeating and occasional ordering, reduces distortion from isolated bulk orders and selects improvements using bounded rolling-origin RMSE checks without looking ahead. Existing data is sufficient; there is no new service or setup.

Forecast leads follow actual week dates, each coffee trains from its first positive sale, and future total grams exactly match the sum of the coffee predictions. Estimated ranges use historical prediction errors with explicit limited-history fallbacks. Insights shows the current unfinished week separately, excludes it from training and reports historical test error. The unverified 80% coverage wording is removed.

Existing reports, records, import cutover, access restrictions and open-order/stock/batch rules remain unchanged. No migration, dependency, secret, external service or DNS change. Unknown history coverage and ambiguous mappings still constrain the forecast; historical checks do not promise future accuracy.
